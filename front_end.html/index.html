About Me

Care that listens before it treats

I'm Ishwari — currently in my final year of Physiotherapy, training under experienced clinicians while building a practice rooted in empathy and evidence. I believe recovery isn't just about exercises; it's about understanding each person's story, lifestyle, and goals.

My focus areas include musculoskeletal pain, post-injury rehabilitation, posture correction, and sports physiotherapy — always with a calm, patient-first approach.

  • Final-year BPT student, clinical training in progress
